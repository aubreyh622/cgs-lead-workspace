## Current product surfaces
- HubSpot provides a Sales Workspace with a prospecting queue that keeps tasks, sales activities, and guided actions on one page.
- Company records can open in a right-side panel with Summary, Insights, People, and Activities views.
- The Prospecting Agent can monitor companies for intent signals, recommend contacts, explain reasons to reach out, and prepare outreach for review.
- Prospecting Agent plays are configured through Audience, Context, Outreach, Guardrails, and Automation.
- Agent Hub provides a consolidated view of active agents, their activity, and their reported outcomes across go-to-market functions.

## Interaction patterns relevant to CGS
- Keep a persistent operating workspace rather than sending users through disconnected pages.
- Pair a prioritized work list with contextual record details and an explicit next action.
- Separate agent activity, evidence, and human approval instead of presenting automation as autonomous completion.
- Allow work to continue manually when an agent is unavailable.
- Use saved views and quick filters to move between operational cohorts.

## Design boundary
- HubSpot is a reference for workspace architecture and interaction patterns only.
- The CGS prototype must retain its own visual identity, terminology, evidence model, and human-decision controls.
- No HubSpot logo, proprietary artwork, screenshots, colors, or copied interface composition will be embedded in the CGS artifact.
